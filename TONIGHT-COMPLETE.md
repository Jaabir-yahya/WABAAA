### **Why Hybrid Approach?**

**Combines Best of Both Worlds**:
- ✅ **Validation-first** (safe, merchant-driven) 
- ✅ **Research-driven** (fast, addresses known pain points)
- ✅ **Balanced risk** (validate before big investment)
- ✅ **Sustainable pace** (3 features, not 8)

### **8-Week Timeline**

```
Week 1-2: Deploy & Onboard 3 merchants
          ↓
Week 3:   Validate top pain points (research-guided questions)
          ↓
Week 4-6: Build top 3 validated features
          ↓
Week 7-8: Iterate & onboard 2 more merchants
          ↓
Result:   5 merchants, 3 features, 90%+ retention
```

### **Validation Criteria (Week 3)**

**Build IF**:
- ✅ 2+ merchants explicitly request it
- ✅ Clear pain point (costs money/time)
- ✅ 5-7 days to build
- ✅ Measurable ROI

**Rule of 2** (not Rule of 3) - faster for pilot phase

### **Top 3 Features** (Research + Validation)

**1. Chama/Group Orders** 🔥 HIGH PRIORITY
- 8M+ Kenyans in chamas
- Group orders = 20-30% of merchant revenue
- Split payment tracking, bulk pricing, group receipts

**2. Supplier Credit Tracking** 🔥 HIGH PRIORITY
- Cash flow killer (merchants forget payments)
- Causes stock-outs when suppliers refuse credit
- Reminders, payment tracking, debt dashboard

**3. Bulk SMS Campaigns** 🔥 HIGH PRIORITY
- WhatsApp limits (1000 messages/day)
- SMS unlimited (KES 0.80 per message)
- Customer segmentation, cost calculator
