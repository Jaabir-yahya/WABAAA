# Kenya Commerce OS: Data Promise

This is our simple promise to every merchant using Kenya Commerce OS.
Tunaandika ahadi yetu kwa uwazi, sio lugha ya kisheria.

---

## What We Store
- Orders and payments made through the system
- Customer phone numbers used for business communication
- Messages and receipts needed to solve disputes

## What We Never Do
- We do not sell your data
- We do not send spam to your customers
- We do not share your data with third parties for marketing

## Your Control
- You can export all your data anytime (CSV + JSON)
- You can stop using the system and take your data with you

## Security Basics
- Data is stored in Supabase with encryption at rest
- Each business is isolated (Row Level Security)
- We keep an audit log for disputes (append-only events)

## If We Shut Down
- We give at least 30 days notice
- We provide a full export of your data

## WhatsApp Rules We Respect
- We follow Meta policies to avoid account suspension
- We avoid spam and respect opt-out requests
- We keep automated messages during business hours
