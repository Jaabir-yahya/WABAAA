# Kenya Commerce OS: Simple Merchant Agreement

This is a simple agreement for pilot merchants. It is short and clear.

---

## What We Provide
- A tool to organize orders and payments
- WhatsApp message logging and simple order tracking
- Offline-first usage with sync when internet returns

## What We Do Not Provide
- We are not a payment processor
- We do not control your M-Pesa or WhatsApp accounts
- We do not guarantee third-party uptime (Meta/Safaricom)

## Your Responsibilities
- Keep your WhatsApp and M-Pesa credentials safe
- Confirm your customer and payment details
- Use the system in good faith (no spam or abuse)

## Data Ownership
- You own your data
- You can export all data anytime (CSV + JSON)
- If you stop, you keep your data

## Pilot Pricing
- Pilot phase is free
- After pilot: KSh 500 per month (subject to agreement)

## Cancellation
- You can cancel anytime with 7 days notice
- We provide a full data export on cancellation

## Acceptance
By using Kenya Commerce OS in the pilot, you accept these simple terms.
