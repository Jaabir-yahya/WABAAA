### Priority 1: Chama Integration ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Problem**: Group orders (churches, schools, offices) are high-value but hard to track  
**Impact**: +20-30% revenue  
**Effort**: 2 weeks  
**ROI**: 500%  

### Priority 2: Supplier Credit Tracking ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Problem**: Merchants forget supplier debts → credit loss → stock-outs  
**Impact**: -25% stock-outs, saves relationships  
**Effort**: 1.5 weeks  
**ROI**: 300%  

### Priority 3: Bulk SMS Campaigns ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Problem**: WhatsApp limits (1000/day) prevent marketing  
**Impact**: +20-40% repeat customers  
**Effort**: 1.5 weeks  
**ROI**: 400%  

### Priority 4: KRA PIN & Tax Tracking ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Problem**: Merchants forget tax deadlines → KES 10K-100K penalties  
**Impact**: Saves KES 30K/year  
**Effort**: 1 week  
**ROI**: 600%  

### Priority 5: County License Tracking ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Problem**: License renewal forgotten → business closure risk  
**Impact**: Saves KES 15K/year  
**Effort**: 1 week  
**ROI**: 500%  

### Priority 6: Fuliza Detection ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Impact**: -15-20% cancellations

## 🎓 KEY INSIGHTS

### What Makes Kenya Different
1. **Chama culture**: 8M+ people in rotating savings groups (unique to East Africa)
2. **Fuliza ubiquity**: 30M+ users rely on overdraft (different from West)
3. **Compliance burden**: KRA PIN, county licenses (easy to forget, expensive penalties)
4. **SMS preference**: WhatsApp has limits, SMS is unlimited and trusted
5. **M-Pesa dominance**: 95% of digital payments (not credit cards)
6. **Agent banking**: Many merchants double as M-Pesa agents (dual income)
7. **Voice notes**: 40% of orders come as WhatsApp voice messages
8. **Group orders**: Churches, schools, offices = bulk revenue

### What NOT to Build
- ❌ Advanced analytics (merchants want traffic lights, not charts)
- ❌ Social media integration (WhatsApp dominates, ignore Instagram/Facebook)
- ❌ Blockchain/Web3 (zero merchant demand)
- ❌ Multi-currency (99% transact in KES only)
- ❌ API for developers (delays merchant value)

### What MUST Be Built (Q1 2026)
- ✅ Chama integration (group orders are 20-30% of revenue)
- ✅ Supplier credit tracking (prevents stock-outs)
- ✅ Bulk SMS (WhatsApp limits prevent marketing)
- ✅ KRA/license tracking (saves KES 10K-50K/year)
- ✅ Fuliza detection (prevents 15-20% cancellations)
